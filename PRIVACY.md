# AutoApply Privacy Policy

AutoApply stores the profiles you create (name, contact details, education, work history, etc.)
in `chrome.storage.local` on your own device. It does not transmit, sync, sell or share any
data, has no servers, analytics or telemetry, and makes no network requests.

Profile data is read only when you click "Fill this page", and is written into form fields on
the active tab only. The data is stored unencrypted in the browser profile; anyone with access
to your browser profile can read it. Remove it any time by deleting profiles in the popup or
uninstalling the extension.
